# OpenEA v1 — G2 exponent-stabilization diagnostics (Patch 14)

## Why Patch 14 is necessary

The existing numerical `assess_stabilization_series` treated a flat finite-
Gaussian energy curve plus approved root labels as enough for `STABLE_BOUND`
(or `STABLE_UNBOUND`). This is insufficient for an automatic **electron-
continuum exclusion** decision. Pseudocontinuum eigenstates can create
apparently stationary plateaus, near-degeneracies and avoided crossings.
A plateau can be useful resonance evidence, but is not proof that a molecular
anion is a square-integrable, asymptotically bound electron-attached state.

Patch 14 changes this: **a separate CLEARED continuum-discrimination review
is mandatory for every G2-effective stabilization classification**, whether
bound or unbound. It is not generated by the exponent-scan executor or by the
AO 1p root-overlap proposal. If absent, `assess_stabilization_series` returns
`UNRESOLVED`, even for an arbitrarily flat curve with EA strictly above zero.
An `UNRESOLVED` review cannot be bypassed by marking root identity `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

Do not set `continuum_discrimination_review` from a `G2_EOM:` raw checkpoint,
`G2_ROOT_PROPOSAL:` or `G2_STABILIZATION_PROFILE:`. Those are diagnostic
provenance, not a separately validated physics review. Separate independent
continuum evidence and its limitations must be inspected and documented.

## Profile analyzer

`adaptive/attachment_stabilization_profile.py` analyzes the complete
`G2EOMSeries` and `RootContinuityReport` without any additional electronic
structure computations:

- Uses exactly one parent augmentation level, the same neutral state,
  geometry, source-checkpoint digest, backend and reviewed shell selectors.
- Contains the *unscaled baseline* explicitly and expects scans on **both**
  sides of factor 1; only pre-authorized exponent-scaled EOM calculations
  are used. No new implicit SCF/CCSD/EOM jobs are requested.
- Uses the proposed (not yet certified) root indices, never ordinal root
  indices or energetically closest roots as substitutes for state continuity.
- Measures the energy span (including the baseline), finite-difference slope
  against **log exponent scale**, and distance to the closest other calculated
  EA-EOM root. Missing or ambiguous paths fail closed.
- Flags inadequate scaling coverage, unusually large finite-scan drift,
  and near-degeneracy with other EOM roots under *configurable diagnostic*
  thresholds. These are **warning thresholds**, not general molecular binding
  criteria and not calibrated uncertainty intervals.
- Emits a deterministic `G2_STABILIZATION_PROFILE:<sha256>` evidence key and
  **always returns `ReviewStatus.UNRESOLVED` / `boundness_decision=UNRESOLVED`**.

The `evidence_points_from_review` adapter now includes the already-selected
unscaled parent at factor `1.0` alongside scaled points. This is just a
reuse of reviewed upstream EOM data, not a new source of evidence.

## Scientific limitations and next step

A root-followed right-EOM one-particle direction is **not** a full Dyson
orbital or a scattering-state diagnostic. Energy stabilization alone cannot
certify absence of a discretized continuum. Even a near-degeneracy warning
is not proof of resonance, and not all relevant roots are necessarily present
in the finite `nroots` spectrum.

For G2 to resolve diffuse / near-threshold cases, an independent physical
continuum-discrimination strategy and its validation are still required.
Possible future work includes spectroscopic/Dyson transition-density analysis,
explicit additional roots and avoided-crossing tracking, and validated
continuum-capable calculations for appropriate molecular classes. None of
these is silently claimed by Patch 14. SOC and production EA estimates
remain orthogonal to this G2 diagnostic.
